Hardware

Every flown model here is drawn from its craft file: each part is the real game model, placed where it sits on the vehicle, with hidden lines removed. Drag to turn it, scroll or pinch to zoom, and pick a stage or system to light it up. Concepts are drawn from the station and lander plans.

Part models come from Kerbal Space Program and the mods ReStock, Bluedog Design Bureau, Cryogenic Engines, Kerbal Reusability Expansion, Tundra Exploration, kOS, MechJeb, Hullcam VDS and ChromaWorks. They are shown here as outlines for a non-commercial fan project, and every right stays with their authors.

KOSMOS, the Kerbal Organization for Socialist Missions in Outer Space, formerly the KCSP. A Kerbal Space Program career in the Ciro system, not a real space agency.
